How it works

1. Submit as a team, log what you did

When your group submits, each member logs their actual work - same doc, sheet, or slides you already turn in. No new system to learn.

2. AI scores every contribution

Each member's work is read and scored on consistent criteria, so the loudest voice in the group chat stops outranking actual work.

3. Per-member grades, plus the flags

A contribution breakdown the professor can grade on, and proof for the student who carried the team - before marks are final, not after.

Who it's for

Built for college students in graded group projects - especially the ones who carried the team - and professors who suspect the group grade is unfair but have no evidence. Today the fix is eating the grade, begging the prof to mediate, or policing teammates on WhatsApp.
